So... What is RIZAL❓

Rizal, officially the Province of Rizal (Filipino: Lalawigan ng Rizal), is a province in the Philippines located in the Calabarzon region in Luzon. Its capital is the city of Antipolo. It is about 16 kilometers (9.9 mi) east of Manila. The province is named after José Rizal, one of the main national heroes of the Philippines.

It is bordered by Metro Manila to the west, Bulacan to the north, Quezon to the east and Laguna to the southeast.

The province also lies on the northern shores of Laguna de Bay, the largest lake in the country. Rizal is a mountainous province perched on the western slopes of the southern portion of the Sierra Madre mountain range.

📖The History📖

Before becoming a province, the area now known as Rizal was a thriving riverine and lakeshore settlement during the pre-Spanish era, strategically located between Manila Bay and Laguna de Bai, and ruled by Rajah Soliman, nephew of Rajah Lakandula of Tondo. Early communities along the Pasig River and Laguna de Bai were active in trade with Chinese and other Asian neighbors even before Spanish colonization in 1521. In 1571, following the defeat of local rajahs by Miguel Lopez de Legazpi, Captain Juan de Salcedo conquered and organized the towns under Spanish rule, while missionaries such as Father Pedro Chirino established churches and spread Catholicism and Spanish culture.


The region later became known for its historical and cultural significance, including the ancient Angono Petroglyphs dating back to around 1000 BC. During the Spanish era, the area formed part of the provinces of Tondo and La Laguna, and in 1853 several towns were reorganized into the Distrito Politico-Militar de los Montes de San Mateo, laying the groundwork for the eventual creation of Rizal Province.

🍃Rizal NOW🍃

Today, Rizal Province is known as a thriving destination for nature, tourism, and peaceful living. Located just east of Metro Manila, it offers breathtaking views of the Sierra Madre mountains, scenic spots overlooking Laguna de Bay, and popular attractions such as Tanay Highlands and Pinto Art Museum.

The province blends natural beauty with art and culture, especially in Angono, recognized as the Art Capital of the Philippines. With its cool mountain air, growing residential communities, and eco-tourism sites, Rizal has become not only a favorite getaway for adventure seekers and families but also a welcoming home for many who seek a balance between city convenience and provincial tranquility.
